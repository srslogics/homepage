The requirement

What the business needed.

  • Connected recordsBilling, stock movements, and payments needed to be reviewed together.
  • Party balancesReceivables and payables needed a traceable view of transactions and balances.
  • Daily reportingThe owner needed an operating view built around the business, rather than a generic accounting template.
The delivery

What SrS Logics built.

  • Retail billingItem shortcuts, invoice previews, and payment handling.
  • Daily entries and ledgersPurchases, sales, payments, stock counts, and party transaction histories.
  • Reports and analyticsExcel and PDF report exports, sales and purchase trends, and payment breakdowns.
