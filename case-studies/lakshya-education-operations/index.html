Project overview

The requirement was not to digitise a spreadsheet. It was to preserve the meaning of existing records inside a controlled operating system.

Lakshya Institute needed more than a standard student-management product. Its admissions, fee handling, batch structure, academic work, faculty access, attendance process, and parent communication followed institute-specific rules that generic software could not represent accurately. SrS Logics designed a unified platform around those real requirements.

One connected source

Management, students, parents, faculty, and the attendance desk receive separate experiences while working from one controlled source of institutional data.

The challenge
  • Distributed recordsStudent, fee, attendance, and admission information had to be checked across multiple workbooks and manual records.
  • Institute-specific rulesActive, cancelled, and opted-out students required different financial treatment, while attendance followed Tatva and Essential batch structures.
  • Different user boundariesManagement, faculty, students, parents, and attendance staff needed separate permissions and interfaces.
  • Historical traceabilityExisting source records had to remain verifiable after migration without exposing confidential student information publicly.
The solution
  • Unified institutional dataAdmissions, finance, academics, attendance, communication, inventory, and reporting now connect through one controlled data model.
  • Role-based applicationsEach user group receives only the tools and information required for its responsibilities.
  • Auditable operating rulesFinancial changes, access decisions, attendance events, and administrative actions remain traceable.
  • Workflow-led interfaceFrequent administrative tasks are organised for faster record lookup, pending-work review, and action completion.
Five connected applications

Every user receives a focused workspace without gaining access to unrelated institutional information.

Management and operations

Admissions, student records, fee ledgers, attendance review, examinations, faculty allocation, timetables, inventory, notices, user access, reporting, and audit history.

Student application

Schedules, assignments, attendance, examinations, results, notices, academic communication, and account controls without sensitive management data.

Parent application

Linked-student attendance, academics, examinations, schedules, notices, relevant account information, and controlled institute communication.

Faculty application

Assigned batches and subjects, schedules, assignments, examinations, marks entry, notices, and student-specific communication within authorised scope.

Attendance desk

Dedicated Tatva and Essential batch rosters with timestamped attendance marking, separated from faculty permissions.

Data migration and reconciliation
  • Legacy references preservedOriginal source context remains available for audit and later verification.
  • Admissions standardisedRecords were separated by status and connected to generated admission numbers, batches, subjects, and attendance.
  • Payments normalisedCondensed descriptions were interpreted as transactions, payment modes were standardised, and unknown dates remained explicitly unknown.
  • Exceptions stayed reviewableMissing information was never silently invented; records requiring clarification remain available for manual completion.
Migration principle

Convert the source into structured application data without rewriting history or hiding uncertainty.

Financial control

A student ledger designed for agreements, payments, balances, future plans, adjustments, and reconciliation.

The finance module preserves what happened historically while allowing management to handle current liability accurately.

01
Preserve money receivedExisting receipts and the original fee agreement remain intact when a student opts out.
02
Close only unpaid liabilityAn auditable adjustment removes unpaid future liability from active totals without deleting history.
03
Cancel future schedulesPlanned payments are closed when access and enrolment status change.
04
Disable linked accessStudent and parent accounts are invalidated when the student is no longer active.
05
Retain the ledgerManagement can still review the complete transaction and adjustment history.
06
Reverse without rewritingIf the student rejoins, a corresponding reversal restores the account while preserving previous transactions.
Inventory integration

Student material distribution now updates stock automatically.

Books, bags, T-shirts, and other materials can be issued or returned from a student profile. Every movement updates available stock while retaining a transaction history.

Security and access control

Access follows responsibility, not convenience.

Separate applications, mobile-number authentication, mandatory first-login password changes, owner-controlled accounts, protected APIs, session invalidation, immutable finance entries, recorded administrative changes, and restricted announcements protect institutional information.
