Website use

Information for evaluation, not an automatic promise.

Accuracy

SrS Logics aims to keep website information accurate and current, but service descriptions, indicative ranges, timelines, and availability may change. A written project document takes priority.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the website, interfere with its operation, attempt unauthorised access, submit unlawful material, or copy protected content beyond what applicable law permits.

External links

Links to scheduling, social, client, or third-party services are provided for convenience. Those services operate under their own terms, availability, and privacy practices.

Project agreements

The signed scope should answer the questions that matter.

  • Deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, and dependencies
  • Milestones, review responsibilities, and acceptance criteria
  • Fees, taxes, currency, invoices, and payment timing
  • Hosting, third-party services, support, and change control
Intellectual property

Ownership is defined per engagement.

Project-specific documents should state what is transferred after payment, what remains client-owned, what pre-existing SrS Logics material is licensed, and how third-party or open-source components are treated. The website itself does not promise a universal ownership model.

Commercial clarity

No estimate should be read without its assumptions.

Indicative pricing

Website ranges are directional only. A quotation becomes meaningful after requirements, data, integrations, infrastructure, timing, and responsibilities are understood.

Delivery timing

Published timelines describe focused delivery paths, not a guarantee for every system. Complexity, client review speed, dependencies, and scope changes affect timing.

Confidential information

Do not send passwords, production credentials, regulated data, or confidential files through an initial website enquiry. Secure exchange and confidentiality terms should be agreed first.

Website availability

Provided on a reasonable-effort basis.

The public website may be changed, interrupted, or withdrawn. To the extent permitted by applicable law, SrS Logics does not warrant uninterrupted availability or suitability of public website content for a specific decision.

Questions

Resolve uncertainty before signing.

If these website terms conflict with a signed project agreement, the signed agreement controls for that engagement. Contact SrS Logics for clarification before relying on an assumption.
