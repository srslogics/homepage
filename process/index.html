Process

A structured delivery process from discovery to handover.

Focused systems can move from discovery to production review in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger platforms are delivered in controlled stages.

Delivery model Scope-dependent
Focused systems A defined MVP or focused operational system may reach production review in 4 to 6 weeks.

Timeline

The standard project path.

Stage 01

Discovery and requirements

Review the client’s workflow, required features, business priorities, and current operating constraints.

Stage 02

Scope and system design

Define the software structure, database model, data movement pattern, and overall delivery approach.

Stage 03

Build and milestone reviews

Build the software modules, APIs, business logic, and key user flows according to the agreed project definition.

Stage 04

QA, acceptance, and handover

Validate agreed workflows, complete client acceptance, document deployment, transfer access, and begin the support period.

Process questions

What clients should expect from delivery through post-launch support.

How long does a project usually take at SrS Logics?

A focused system may move from discovery to production review in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger or integration-heavy platforms use staged timelines defined after discovery.

What are the main stages in the process?

The usual process includes requirement gathering, planning and design, development, testing, final review, handover, and post-launch support.

Is support included after launch?

Proposals can include 6 months of post-launch maintenance. The covered services, response route, and exclusions are stated in the project agreement.
