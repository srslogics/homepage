Before the first conversation

How to prepare a custom software brief

  • Explain the problem or idea and the outcome you want.
  • List who will use the software and what each person needs to do.
  • Describe current tools, required integrations, and records to migrate.
  • Separate essential first-release requirements from later improvements.
  • Note budget, deadlines, access restrictions, and how you will review the result.
No technical specification required

Start with your requirements.

A short explanation of your users and their work is enough to start the discussion. Use anonymised examples rather than sharing passwords, student records, payment details, or other confidential information in an initial enquiry.

Process questions

Reviews, handover, and support.

How long does a project usually take at SS49 D1T1TECH?

A focused system may move from discovery to production review in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger or integration-heavy platforms use staged timelines defined after discovery.

What are the main stages in the process?

The usual process includes requirement gathering, planning and design, development, testing, final review, handover, and post-launch support.

Is support included after launch?

Proposals can include 6 months of post-launch maintenance. The covered services, response route, and exclusions are stated in the project agreement. Bug-fix coverage is defined before launch. Hosting administration, third-party charges, new features, and support after that period are identified separately.
