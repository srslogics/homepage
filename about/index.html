What we build

Software is designed around the business requirement, not around a fixed product template.

SrS Logics works with businesses that need software aligned to their workflow, data handling pattern, operating pace, and internal priorities. Engagements range from operational platforms and finance systems to workflow tools, management dashboards, and client-specific data systems.

Company standard

The objective is not just to build software. It is to build a system that reduces confusion, improves control, and remains usable in daily operations.

Office presence

SrS Logics operates from Nagpur, India, with remote coordination and client-specific delivery support.

Work spans local businesses as well as clients beyond the immediate region. Delivery may involve remote planning, digital review cycles, direct requirement sessions, and client-site coordination where relevant.

Local credibility

The company’s Nagpur work includes deployed systems, active development, and client references across multiple business categories.

Current work includes finance dashboards, venue operations, poultry operations, loyalty workflows, data analysis systems, and other business-specific software. It shows the range of operating problems SrS Logics is already solving.
