Scope and pricing ↗
What does SS49 D1T1TECH build?

SS49 D1T1TECH, formerly SrS Logics, is a custom software development company based in Nagpur, India. We build custom applications, connected business systems, and digital products. We start with your goals, users, and requirements, then agree the features, technology, and delivery plan. Our work is not restricted to one industry. Learn about our company and founder or our change of name.

Where does SS49 D1T1TECH work?

From Nagpur, India. We are available for projects in India, the UAE, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

How long does delivery usually take?

A focused first release may reach production review in 4 to 6 weeks. Larger platforms use staged timelines.

Is support included after launch?

Proposals can include 6 months of post-launch maintenance. The covered services, response route, and exclusions are stated in the project agreement. See our delivery and support process.

Why not use generic SaaS?

Use ready-made SaaS when it fits. Custom software becomes useful when your roles, approvals, data, or operating rules do not. Compare the options in our custom software decision guide.

Can I see software you have delivered?

Our deployed work includes KNP Signature for billing, stock, payments, and ledgers; Royal Celebration Console for venue operations; and Lakshya Institute's education platform. Explore the client case studies and project register for screenshots and current delivery statuses.
