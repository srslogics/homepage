Typical investment

A useful starting point for your budget.

India engagements commonly begin within an indicative ₹30,000 to ₹5 lakh range. Broader institutional systems are typically scoped at ₹6 to ₹15 lakh. These are different levels of work, not fixed packages or upper limits.

A focused workflow

One process, such as an approval flow or a reporting dashboard. A focused release may reach production review in 4 to 6 weeks, depending on scope. Users, inputs, and acceptance checks are agreed first.

A connected business system

Related workflows such as billing, stock, and ledgers, with roles, shared records, and data migration. The quote depends on the operating rules and integrations; broader systems use staged releases.

A multi-role platform

Separate staff, customer, or partner workspaces, with permissions and integrations. Broader institutional systems typically require 16 to 18 weeks. Review institutional scope and investment.

The full commitment

Know the build cost and the running costs.

Development and handover

The proposal identifies features, migration, integrations, testing, training, and access transfer. Included work and exclusions are listed before you commit.

Hosting and external services

Hosting, domains, messaging, and paid integrations may carry recurring charges. The proposal states which apply, who pays them, and who manages the accounts.

Support and future changes

Proposals can include 6 months of post-launch maintenance. Bug-fix coverage, response arrangements, and exclusions are agreed in writing. New features and later support are scoped separately.

International projects are quoted in GBP, USD, or AED. UAE guidance covers indicative AED ranges; the written quote defines your actual scope and currency.

Pricing questions

Pricing questions.

What is the typical software pricing range at SS49 D1T1TECH?

India engagements commonly begin within an indicative ₹30,000 to ₹5 lakh range. This is not a fixed package or an upper limit: larger platforms are scoped separately. International work is quoted in GBP, USD, or AED after the requirement, risk, infrastructure, and delivery responsibilities are understood.

What affects custom software pricing?

Pricing depends on software responsiveness, data movement complexity, local or cloud database setup, workflow depth, and the overall scale of the build.

How are payments usually structured?

Projects generally follow a three-stage payment structure around contract confirmation, build progress, and final handover. Review our development and acceptance process for the stages behind those milestones.

What costs should we consider beyond development?

Ask which hosting, domain, third-party service, migration, training, and maintenance costs apply to your project. The proposal should identify included work, recurring charges, and responsibilities; not every project needs every item.

Can we begin with a smaller first release?

We can review a focused first release around the most important user task. Later features can be planned separately, subject to technical dependencies. Our software development services start with requirements rather than fixed packages.

What should we share to get an estimate?

Describe your users, required actions, existing tools, integrations, and any budget or deadline constraints. Use the project brief checklist to prepare without sending passwords or private customer records.
